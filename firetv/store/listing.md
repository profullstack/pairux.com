# Store listing: PairUX (Fire TV)

- **Title:** pairux
- **Package:** com.pairux.tv
- **Category:** Productivity
- **Price:** Free. No in-app purchases in the Fire TV app. No ads.
- **What it is on a TV:** a viewer. Open a PairUX room on the big screen and watch the
  screen someone else is sharing. Sharing your own screen and taking remote control are

- **Support:** anthony@profullstack.com, https://github.com/profullstack/pairux.com/issues

## Short description

Watch a PairUX screen share on your TV. Open a room by code or link, see what the other person is sharing on the big screen, and drive the whole thing with the remote.

## Long description

PairUX is collaborative screen sharing with simultaneous remote mouse and keyboard control, like Screenhero but open source. This is PairUX on a television: the viewer's half.

- Join a room by code, or follow a room link, and watch the shared screen full size
- Made for the remote: the D-pad moves a clear focus ring, OK activates, Back walks back, and the Menu key asks for a room code from anywhere
- Rooms can be anonymous and end to end encrypted; the key lives in the link, never on a server
- Sign in to reach your own channels and scheduled sessions
- Free and open source (MIT): github.com/profullstack/pairux.com

Sharing your own screen and remote control need a computer: install the PairUX desktop app from pairux.com. The Fire TV app is for watching.
